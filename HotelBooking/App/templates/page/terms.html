Terms and conditions

Lushiux Hotel is a fictional school project. Nothing here is a real offer or contract.

1. Demo use only

This site simulates a hotel booking. No real rooms are reserved and no real payments are taken. Please do not enter real financial or sensitive personal information.

2. Bookings

A room can be booked for one or more nights. Check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. A room that is already occupied cannot be booked until it is released.

3. Pricing

The total is the nightly room rate multiplied by the number of nights, plus any packs you add. Packs are priced per person, per person per morning, or per group, as shown on each pack.

4. Cancellations

You can cancel a booking from My bookings. A cancelled booking makes its room available again.

5. Your information

The details you enter, such as your name and contact information, are used only to show your booking summary. In this demo they may be cleared when the session ends or the data is reset.
