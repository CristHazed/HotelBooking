About Lushiux Hotel

Perched high above a misty tropical rainforest valley, Lushiux Hotel is an ultra-luxury highland sanctuary where untamed jungle adventure meets restorative architectural calm. Designed for travelers seeking true seclusion without sacrificing modern indulgence, our glass-and-timber retreat immerses you directly within the upper forest canopy.

Whether you spend your mornings trekking emerald mountain ridges and bamboo river trails, or your evenings unwinding in heated mineral hydrotherapy pools beneath a mountaintop observatory, every detail of your stay is curated for effortless wonder. At Lushiux Hotel, nature is not merely a backdrop—it is the heart of your escape.
